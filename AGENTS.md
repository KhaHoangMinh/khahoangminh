# Agent Instructions — Kha Hoàng Minh

Before writing or modifying ANY UI code (pages, components, CSS, Tailwind/theme config, or assets), you MUST read and strictly follow the design system rules in **[.agents/rules/](.agents/rules/)**:

1. **[Master Index](.agents/rules/ui-ux-design.md)**: Executive overview and rule map.
2. **[Anti-"AI Slop" & Senior Craftsmanship](.agents/rules/anti-ai-slop.md)**: Strict bans on SaaS clichés (purple glows, floating blobs), blind dark-mode inversion, dead placeholders, and monolithic controller files.
3. **[UI System & Surface Architecture](.agents/rules/ui.md)**: 4-tier dual-theme surface tokens, brushed gunmetal palette, typography contrast matrix, and banned Tailwind classes.
4. **[UX & B2B Buyer Workflows](.agents/rules/ux.md)**: Quotation/RFQ workflows, direct hotlines, form validation, error states, and touch accessibility.
5. **[Code Architecture & Atomic Design](.agents/rules/architecture.md)**: 5-layer atomic pattern (`atoms` → `molecules` → `organisms` → `templates` → thin controllers), barrel imports, and localization DRY.
6. **[Responsiveness & Viewport Budget](.agents/rules/responsiveness.md)**: Mobile-first breakpoint matrix (`360px` to `1920px+`), framing utilities (`.section-frame-half`, `.section-frame-full`, `.section-hero-compact`), and asymmetric layouts.
7. **[Assets & Optical Performance](.agents/rules/performance-assets.md)**: Optical logo normalization, studio-lit platinum stage, Next.js image optimization, and motion reduction.
8. **[Pre-Delivery Quality Checklist](.agents/rules/quality-checklist.md)**: Mandatory 8-point verification checklist and zero-regression gate before finishing any task.
