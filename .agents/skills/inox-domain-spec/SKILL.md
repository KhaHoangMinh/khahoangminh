# KHM Stainless Steel (Inox) Domain Specification Skill

Consult this reference when writing product descriptions, catalog specs, or machining capabilities for Kha Hoàng Minh:

### 1. Certified Stainless Steel Grades
- **SUS 304 / 304L**: Austenitic stainless steel (18Cr-8Ni). Non-magnetic. Exceptional corrosion resistance and formability. Primary grade for food processing, architectural fabrication, and industrial containers.
- **SUS 316 / 316L**: Molybdenum-added austenitic grade. Superior resistance to chlorides and marine/chemical environments.
- **SUS 201**: Austenitic-manganese grade. High tensile strength, economical structural alternative for indoor architecture and furniture framing.
- **SUS 430**: Ferritic stainless steel. Magnetic. Cost-effective option for decorative trims, home appliances, and non-critical industrial panels.

### 2. Surface Finishes & Processing Standards
- **Finishes**: `No.1` (hot-rolled, annealed & pickled), `2B` (cold-rolled, smooth matte), `BA` (Bright Annealed, reflective), `HL` (Hairline brushed), `8K` (mirror finish).
- **Standards**: ASTM A240, JIS G4303, DIN.
- **Machining Services**: CNC Plasma cutting, high-power fiber laser cutting, hydraulic press braking (*chấn gấp*), coil slitting (*xả băng*), and plate shearing (*chặt tấm*).

### 3. Theoretical Weight & Zero-Price Enforcement
